= Agile & Scrum – Assessment Questions for Freshers

== Conceptual Understanding

* What is Agile methodology, and how is it different from a traditional project management approach?
* What are the four key values of the Agile Manifesto?
* How does Agile emphasise "responding to change over following a plan"?

*Agile vs Scrum*

* What is the difference between Agile and Scrum? Explain with examples.
* Name at least two other Agile frameworks apart from Scrum and briefly describe them.

*Scrum Basics*

* What are the core components of the Scrum framework?
* Explain the concept of a sprint and its typical duration.
* What are the three pillars of the empirical process in Scrum?

*Roles & Responsibilities*

* What are the responsibilities of a Product Owner in Scrum?
* What role does a Scrum Master play in a team?
* Who are Developers in Scrum, and what is their key responsibility?

*Scrum Events*

* What is discussed in a Daily Scrum meeting?
* What is the purpose of a Sprint Review and how does it help stakeholders?

*Artifacts & Work Management*

* What is the difference between Product Backlog and Sprint Backlog?
* What is a User Story and how is it different from an Epic?

*Bonus Discussion Questions*

* Why should velocity not be used to compare teams?
* What are some common challenges faced while adopting Scrum?

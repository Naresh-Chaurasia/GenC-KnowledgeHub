= Scrum and Agile Interview Questions

1. *What is Agile methodology?*
   Explain the basic idea behind Agile and how it differs from traditional models like Waterfall.

2. *What is Scrum?*
   Describe Scrum as a framework and its purpose in Agile development.

3. *What are the key roles in Scrum?*
   Explain the responsibilities of Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Development Team.

4. *What is a Sprint?*
   What happens during a Sprint and what is its typical duration?

5. *What is a Product Backlog?*
   Who manages it and what does it contain?

6. *What is a Sprint Backlog?*
   How is it different from the Product Backlog?

7. *What is a Daily Stand-up (Daily Scrum)?*
   What are the three main questions typically answered in this meeting?

8. *What is a Sprint Review?*
   What is its purpose and who participates in it?

9. *What is a Sprint Retrospective?*
   How does it help improve the team’s performance?

10. *What is the concept of “Definition of Done”?*
    Why is it important in Agile projects?
